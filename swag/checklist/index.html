Swag Checklist

We typically have the designer or design firm we are working with produce design files for our swag. Those are as follows:

  • T-Shirt Design
  • Tote Bag Design
  • Sticker Designs
  • Speaker Backdrop/Banners
  • Lanyard Design
  • Nametag Design
  • Keynote/Slideshow Template
  • Speaker Gift (if necessary)

Swag Providers¶

Shirts¶

Previous providers: - Inkfinity - Chicago Signs

Models we typically order: - Bella+Canvas 3001

Tote Bags¶

Previous provider: Bagmasters

  • The Original Custom Cotton Tote with an average final cost of ~$3-4/per piece.
  • Single color silkscreen, single sided
  • Order at least 3-4 weeks before conference

Lanyards¶

Previous provider: Custom Lanyard who has now rebranded as Swag Print

  • 1 inch x 36 inch nylon lanyards, screen printed
  • Single bulldog clip with standard sewing and flat plastic breakaway
  • Order 5% red, 5% yellow and 90% regular colors
  • Final cost should be between $0.90c-$1.40c/per piece
  • Order at least 3-4 weeks before conference

Lanyard Ribbons: Marco Promos

Name Badges¶

Conference Badge can handle both DIY and printed badges.

  • Badge Holders on Amazon
  • Badge Insert Paper on Amazon - account for 2 badges/person

Stickers¶

Previous provider: SierraCustomStickers

Banners and Signs¶

  • 10ft x 7ft Tension Fabric Backdrop
  • 33in x 78in Tension Fabric Stand
  • 24in x 18in Foam Boards

Other Things To Order¶

  • Thank you cards for speakers and volunteers, sometimes sponsors
  • Speaker gifts, something small that can be easily taken home
  • Toiletries for shared/public bathrooms
