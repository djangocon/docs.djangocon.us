# Code of Conduct Checklist

- [ ] Add photography policy to site
- [ ] Confirm CoC is on site 
- [ ] Add CoC contacts to site, with phone numbers 
- [ ] Add CoC contact email to site 

## Once website is up

- [ ] Review code of conduct page. Add city relevant information or review if it's the second year in that city. Confirm members of the conduct team and verify that their email is on the list for conduct@djangocon.us. Make any updates via that year's website github. For reference here is the 2026 [CoC page](https://2026.djangocon.us/conduct/) and [website github](https://github.com/djangocon/2026.djangocon.us).

## 1 month out
- [ ] Make sure some posts go out on social media around conference letting people know about code of conduct. Here is an [example post](https://bsky.app/profile/djangocon.us/post/3monsx7qusn2k).

## During conference

- [ ] Monitor slack and email for reports of conduct violations
- [ ] Put out messaging to remind people how to report any concerns. This should be done via social media and signage. [Here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/125NChRcI6zL4wgRo5bfWhHgZhIGZeSWRw-WZDbCmGsc/edit?usp=sharing) is an example of planned signage messaging from 2025.

## After conference

- [ ] Put together the transparency report with info on any incidents. Here are the reports from [2025](https://2025.djangocon.us/news/code-of-conduct-transparency-report/) and [2024](https://2024.djangocon.us/news/code-of-conduct-transparency-report/).
